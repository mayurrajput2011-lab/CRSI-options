Candle patterns cover TradingView's full Screener taxonomy (Abandoned Baby, Tri-Star, Rising/Falling Three Methods, Tasuki Gaps, On-Neck, Long Shadows, and all the standard reversal/continuation patterns) — each shown with its icon plus a Strength (Strong/Medium/Weak) and Type (Reversal/Continuation) tag. Setup Score is a 0–100 technical confluence score (CRSI extremity + candle clarity + ATR expansion + volume) — it is NOT a statistical probability of profit. Your Win Rate is pulled from YOUR OWN closed trades in this journal that share the same candle pattern & ATR state (shown only once you have 3+ matching trades) — your real personal track record, not a market-wide guarantee. This is simulated tracking only — clicking Buy or Sell on the Screener does not place a real order anywhere. Entry price is the screener's latest close at the moment you clicked. Open positions are marked to market against the latest available daily close every time you open this page or click Refresh Prices, so P&L updates automatically — refresh tomorrow and it'll reflect tomorrow's close. A trade never auto-closes; it stays OPEN until you click Exit. Day 1–Day 5 are auto-fetched, same as your manual journal's D1–D5 fields, but pulled straight from real market data instead of typed in by hand — Day 1 is the next trading day's close after entry, and so on; blank means that day hasn't happened yet. Buy simulates going long; Sell simulates going short. Clicking Exit opens a dialog pre-filled with the latest fetched price — confirm it as-is or type your own to override — plus an optional exit note. Click any card to expand the full detail panel (candle metrics, CRSI/ATR values, volume, day-by-day moves) and jot down your own notes, same as the Journal's Learnings field. Naming a date in the sidebar (✏️) is shared with the Journal — name a day once, see it in both places. This reflects daily-close prices, not real intraday execution, so treat it as a directional signal test rather than a precise fill simulation.

How this works: for every day in the chosen history, the same CRSI/ATR/candle filters used in the Screener are checked using only data available up to that day (no lookahead). The first day everything matches, a trade opens at that day's close — in the candle's own direction if Trade Side is "Auto", or forced to Buy/Sell regardless of candle color if you pick one explicitly (useful for testing a directional bias independent of what triggers it). It's held as long as each following day closes in its favor (or flat); the moment a day closes against it, the trade exits at that close. Only one trade is open at a time. This uses daily closes, not intraday fills, and assumes you could always enter/exit exactly at the close — a simplification, not a guarantee of real execution. Past performance of a rule like this says nothing certain about future results.
